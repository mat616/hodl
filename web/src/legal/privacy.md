## 2. The short version

- We do not ask for your name, email address or identity documents.
- We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
- Your browser stores one item for us: a record that you accepted the terms.
- We store the public wallet address you connect, so the keeper can find your approvals.
- The website loads fonts, token images and blockchain data directly from other companies. They see your IP address (section 6).
- Everything you do on-chain is public and permanent. We cannot change or delete it.

## 3. What we collect

**Wallet address.** After you accept the terms and connect a wallet, the website sends its public address to our API. If you return with the same browser and wallet, the website reconnects and sends it again without asking. We store it, with the time it was first seen, in a list of wallets the keeper checks for token approvals. We also use it to show your balances, allowance, stake and history.

**On-chain activity.** We read and keep copies of public blockchain data relating to the program: contributions, weights, sweeps, draws, winners, refunds and the reward payouts that funded them. This data is linked to wallet addresses.

**Technical logs.** Our servers and hosting provider log requests to the website and API. Logs include IP address, time, the address requested (which can include your wallet address), and browser information.

**Terms acceptance.** Your browser's local storage keeps the version of the terms you accepted. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

**Messages you send us.** If you email us, we keep the message and your address.

We do not collect private keys or seed phrases. We never ask for them.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not build advertising profiles.
- We do not currently run identity checks, location blocking or VPN detection. See section 9.

## 6. Who receives it

- **Hosting.** The operator's host processes technical logs and stored data on our behalf.
- **Solana RPC providers.** Your browser sends transactions and requests, including your wallet address, directly to the RPC endpoint configured for the page. That provider sees your IP address. The API uses its own RPC endpoint.
- **Fonts.** The website loads fonts from Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`). Google sees your IP address and browser details.
- **Token images.** Token icons may load from a third-party host. That host sees your IP address and browser details.
- **Your wallet provider.** Phantom handles connection and signing under its own privacy policy.
- **Other services our servers call.** A randomness oracle, price and swap APIs, and an optional rewards API. These calls do not include your IP address. Some include public wallet or transaction data.
- **The public.** The API returns pot data, including contributing wallet addresses, amounts and winners, to anyone. The same data is public on-chain.
- **Authorities and advisers.** We disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights or users.
- **Successors.** If the Service is transferred to another operator, the information goes with it under this policy.

## 7. Blockchain data

Transactions on Solana are public, permanent and outside our control. Anyone can link the activity of a wallet address across services. If a wallet address can be tied to you, your activity in the pots can be too. We cannot edit or erase blockchain records.

## 12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict our use of it, and to complain to your data protection authority.

To use these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from an address we can reply to and tell us the wallet address concerned. We may ask you to sign a message with the wallet to prove you control it.

There is no self-service removal. On request we remove your wallet address from our watch list by hand and delete our logs. Two limits apply:

- If you connect that wallet to the website again, it is added again.
- While a token approval to the program exists on-chain, the keeper can still find and sweep it without the watch list. Revoke the approval to stop that.

We cannot remove or alter data on the blockchain, and public pot history will continue to show the addresses that took part.
